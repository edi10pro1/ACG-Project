# The Age of Dragons
## The Premise
Thanks to Hiccup, his village has realized that dragons and humans can coexist in harmony. 
However, there is still a long way to go and a lot to learn. So, watch Hiccup and his friends (Toothless included) 
as they discover new dragons and how to train them.
This game is inspired by "Dreamworks: Dragons", 
the series made after the first movie of "How To Train Your Dragon", broadcasted on Cartoon Network between 2012 and 2014.
